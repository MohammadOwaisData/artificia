About Pro Solar

Clean Energy For All, Backed By Proven Delivery

Pro Solar is a Pakistan-based solar energy and energy storage company. We design, install and maintain solar photovoltaic systems for homes, shops, offices, factories and industrial facilities.

Our business is built on three things: honest system sizing, quality installation practice and dependable after-sales support through the ProCare customer care service.

  • Accurate system sizing based on real consumption patterns
  • On-grid, hybrid, off-grid and energy storage capability
  • Residential, commercial and industrial project experience
  • Ongoing O&M, cleaning and remote monitoring support
