About Pro Solar

Clean Energy For All — a Pakistan-based solar energy and energy storage company delivering complete systems for homes, businesses and industry.

Who We Are

A Solar Energy And Energy Storage Company, Built Around Real Performance

Pro Solar works across the full lifecycle of a solar project. That means we are involved long before the panels reach a roof: assessing the property, understanding how electricity is actually used, sizing the system correctly, installing it properly, and then staying available afterwards through our ProCare customer care service.

Our customers range from individual homeowners looking to reduce a monthly bill, to shops and offices that need power through long operating hours, to factories and industrial facilities that depend on stable, continuous supply. The technology differs, but the standard we hold ourselves to does not: correct engineering, clean workmanship and support that actually responds.

  • Solar PV systems and battery energy storage systems under one roof
  • On-grid, hybrid and off-grid architectures
  • Residential, commercial and industrial delivery capability

Our Story

From Installation To Complete Energy Solutions

Pro Solar started by doing what solar companies in Pakistan were doing at the time: installing panels. Over time the work changed. Customers stopped asking only for panels and started asking whether the system would actually reduce the bill, whether power would stay on during an outage, and who they would call when something stopped producing.

That shift is where Pro Solar came from. We now operate as a complete solar solutions company — design, installation, maintenance, cleaning, remote monitoring and configuration — with energy storage built into our offer from the start rather than bolted on later.

Today our work spans homes, shops, offices, shopping complexes, factories and industrial facilities. The projects are bigger, but the promise is the same: honest sizing, proper installation, and support after handover.
