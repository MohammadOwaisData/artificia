How To Read These Numbers

Connected loadRunning power, in watts
Daily / monthly useEnergy consumed, in kWh
System sizeGeneration capacity, in kWp
Panels neededModules at the rating you chose
Battery storageCapacity for your backup window

Connected load tells you whether your supply is single-phase or three-phase. Consumption tells you how much energy solar has to replace. Solar sizing follows consumption, not connected load.

Add an appliance above to see your estimate. Results update instantly as you change any rating, quantity or usage hour.
Disclaimer: these results are automated estimates for guidance, not a final engineering design. They depend on the ratings and usage hours you enter and on the site assumptions you select above. Final sizing, protection, cable selection and battery specification must be confirmed by Pro Solar after a proper technical and site assessment.

Questions About The Estimate

                    
Short answers to the things people usually ask when they first use
                        the calculator.

                

                

                    

                        

                            

                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Connected load is the total power your equipment draws at one moment, measured
                                    in watts or kilowatts. Energy consumption is how much electricity you actually
                                    use over a period of time, measured in kilowatt-hours. A 1000 W iron used for
                                    30 minutes is 0.5 kWh of energy. Solar sizing follows energy consumption.
                                

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    It is a directional estimate. The calculator divides your daily consumption by
                                    the peak sun hours you select and applies a performance factor for real-world
                                    losses. Your actual system depends on roof area, orientation, shading, soiling,
                                    cable losses and your specific equipment.
                                

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Connected load is the deciding factor. The calculator flags three-phase once
                                    your total connected load goes beyond roughly 10 kW, which is the usual
                                    threshold for a single-phase supply in Pakistan. Shops, offices and
                                    commercial facilities often land here.

                                    window — the calculator turns your daily energy use into a storage
                                    figure. For anything critical, we size storage from the backup load during
                                    a real assessment.
