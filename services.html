Services / EPC

Complete solar solutions covering design, supply, installation, commissioning, maintenance, cleaning and remote monitoring.

What We Do

Six Services That Cover The Whole System Lifecycle

Whether you need a single rooftop system or a full energy solutions engagement, these are the services that make up a Pro Solar project.

Service 01

Complete Solar Solutions

End-to-end solar energy solutions for residential, commercial and industrial customers. Pro Solar takes responsibility for the whole project rather than handing you between separate suppliers: assessment, engineering, equipment supply, installation, electrical integration, commissioning, documentation and ongoing support.

  • Single point of contact for the complete project
  • Coordinated design, procurement, installation and handover
  • Suitable for homes, shops, offices, complexes, factories and facilities
  • Solar PV and battery energy storage handled together
Service 02

Solar System Designing & Consultation

Professional solar system design, sizing and consultation. We start from actual consumption data and your site conditions, then work out how much generation is genuinely needed, where the array should sit, how it should be protected, and whether storage is justified.

  • Load analysis from bills and equipment schedules
  • Solar resource, shading and roof/land assessment
  • Array layout, string design and protection coordination
  • Solar versus storage economics explained in plain terms
Service 03

Solar System Installation

Installation and commissioning of solar PV systems and associated equipment, carried out by trained technicians. How the array is mounted and how the electrical work is done determines how long the system lasts and how well it performs, so this is where most of our care goes.

  • Structural suitability and safe mounting installation
  • Correct DC/AC protection, earthing and cable management
  • Inverter and battery installation where applicable
  • Commissioning, testing and system handover documentation
Service 04

Solar Systems Maintenance

Ongoing maintenance and technical support to keep your system producing as designed. Panels degrade, connections loosen, components fail and performance drifts — regular attention is what keeps output predictable.

  • Routine preventive maintenance visits
  • Performance checks against expected generation
  • Fault diagnosis and component replacement
  • Technical support through the ProCare helpline
Service 05

Solar System Cleaning Services

Professional solar panel cleaning. In dusty conditions, even a thin layer of deposited dust on modules can reduce output noticeably, and hot spots from dirty cells can shorten panel life. Cleaning restores the performance the system was designed to deliver.

  • Panel, frame and accessible surface cleaning
  • Safe access working on rooftops and elevated structures
  • Removal of accumulated dust, leaves and bird droppings
  • Recommended cleaning frequency based on site conditions
Service 06

Remote Monitoring & Configurations

Remote monitoring, performance analysis, troubleshooting and configuration support for compatible systems. Where the equipment allows it, being able to see what a system is actually doing from Pro Solar's side is the fastest way to catch a developing fault.

  • Generation and consumption data review
  • Fault alerts and underperformance identification
  • Inverter, charge controller and battery configuration changes
  • Support for monitoring platforms used on the installed system
EPC Process

How A Pro Solar Project Runs

01. Consultation

We discuss your property, consumption, tariff and what you want solar to achieve for you.

02. Site Survey

A physical survey of the roof, structure, shading and electrical arrangement.

03. Design & Proposal

A written design with the sizing logic, equipment selection and expected performance.

04. Installation

Structured installation and commissioning by trained technicians.

05. Handover

Commissioning records, system documentation and warranty terms explained.

06. ProCare Support

Ongoing after-sales support, maintenance and monitoring for the life of the system.
